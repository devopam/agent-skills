# Pack: privacy-py (Paraguay)

**Status: LIMITED — no general PDP law**

Paraguay has **Ley 6534/2020** focused on **credit information**, not a comprehensive personal data protection code. Broader protection relies on constitutional habeas data and sectoral rules.

## Skill behaviour
1. Do not present a general PDP obligation card set  
2. If user scope is credit bureaux / credit data only, orient to Ley 6534  
3. Otherwise **Not covered as general privacy regime**
