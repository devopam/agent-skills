# Pack: privacy-gl (Greenland)

**Status: SPECIAL — not EU GDPR territory**

Greenland is **not** covered by an EU adequacy decision in the same way as Faroe. Transfers from EU/EEA often require Chapter V GDPR tools (Art. 46 etc.). Local Greenlandic rules apply for processing in Greenland — **verify current primary** at scan time.

## Skill behaviour
Do not assume GDPR applies as local law in Greenland. For EU→Greenland transfers, orient to transfer tools. For local processing, cite verified Greenlandic primary only.

**Runnable:** limited / transfer-aware
