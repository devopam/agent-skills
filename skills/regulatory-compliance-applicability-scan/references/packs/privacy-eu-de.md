# Pack: privacy-eu-de (Germany overlay)

**Pair with:** `privacy-eu`  
**Primary national:** Bundesdatenschutzgesetz (**BDSG**) 2018 as amended  
**Official orientation:** https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bdsg_2018/  
**Authority:** BfDI + Länder DPAs (one-stop-shop via GDPR)

## Article-level national margins

| Theme | Primary |
|-------|---------|
| Employment data | **§ 26 BDSG** — processing for employment relationship; crime detection threshold; special categories; collective agreements (Art. 88 GDPR opening) |
| Special categories / public interest openings | **§ 22 BDSG** (and related) — national Art. 9 openings |
| Video / scoring / credit (where still relevant under BDSG structure) | Check current consolidations — prefer live text |
| Public bodies / specific federal rules | Parts of BDSG governing public-sector processing |

## Skill behaviour
1. Apply GDPR baseline first  
2. If DE employment or other BDSG openings apply, cite **§ 26** (and related) from official text  
3. Do not treat BDSG as replacing GDPR
