# Pack: privacy-ru (Russia — 152-FZ)

**Primary:** Федеральный закон от 27.07.2006 N 152-ФЗ «О персональных данных»  
**Registry:** `ru-152-fz`  
**Official preference:** state publication / pravo.gov.ru consolidations  
**Convenience aid:** Consultant/Garant (not a substitute for official text)  
**Runnable:** yes  
**Language:** Russian authoritative

## Critical caution

152-FZ and related acts **amend frequently** (localization, cross-border
notification, processor duties). Every finding that states a localization or
transfer duty must be labelled **verify current consolidations** — prefer
“unknown / verify” over invented mandatory rules from outdated summaries.

## When to suggest

Russian data subjects; Russian operator; RU-targeted product (.ru, RU language +
RU market signals); 152-FZ / Roskomnadzor mentions.
