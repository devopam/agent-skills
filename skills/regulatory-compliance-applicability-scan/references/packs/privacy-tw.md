# Pack: privacy-tw (Taiwan — PDPA)

**Primary:** 個人資料保護法 (Personal Data Protection Act)  
**Registry:** `tw-pdpa`  
**Official:** https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=I0050021  
**Runnable:** yes  
**Language:** Chinese (Traditional) authoritative

## In-force vs amendment gate (critical)

- Apply the **currently in-force** PDPA text on law.moj.gov.tw.  
- Major amendments have been **promulgated** (including Nov 2025 packages) whose
  **effective date may be undetermined** (Executive Yuan commencement).  
- Do **not** treat not-yet-in-force articles (e.g. new breach-notify, PDPC powers,
  DPO for agencies) as current obligations unless MOJ status shows they are in force.  
- Label pending reforms as **Not assessed / watch commencement**.

## When to suggest

Taiwan data subjects; Taiwan entity; TW-market product; PDPA mentions;
traditional Chinese privacy policy for TW users.
