# Pack: privacy-kw (Kuwait)

**Status: LIMITED / NOT a general PDP code**

Kuwait does **not** have a comprehensive personal data protection law applicable to all controllers. Sectoral instruments exist (e.g. E-Commerce Law 20/2014 privacy aspects; Cybercrime Law 63/2015; CITRA Data Privacy Protection Regulation 2024 for **telecom licensees**).

## Skill behaviour

1. Do **not** present Kuwait as having a GDPR-style general PDP act  
2. If user is a CITRA-licensed telecom provider, may orient to CITRA regulation only with clear sector gate  
3. Otherwise state **Not covered as general privacy regime** — sectoral analysis only if user confirms sector  
4. No invented general obligation cards

**Runnable:** limited (sector-gated only)
