# Pack: privacy-cn (China — PIPL)

**Primary:** Personal Information Protection Law of the PRC (个人信息保护法)  
**Registry id:** `cn-pipl`  
**Authoritative language:** Chinese  
**English:** reference only  
**Runnable:** yes

## Official orientation

Adopted 20 Aug 2021; effective 1 Nov 2021. Re-verify article numbers on
authoritative Chinese text before hard claims.

## When to suggest

PI of natural persons in the PRC; products/services to individuals in the PRC;
analyzing behaviour of individuals in the PRC (Art. 3 extraterritorial triggers).
