# Pack: privacy-cn (China — PIPL)

**Primary:** Personal Information Protection Law of the PRC (个人信息保护法)  
**Registry id:** `cn-pipl`  
**Authoritative language:** Chinese  
**English:** NPC / other translations are **reference only**  
**Runnable:** yes (orientation; verify article numbers against current Chinese text)

## Official orientation

- Adopted 20 Aug 2021; effective 1 Nov 2021  
- English reference (not authoritative): paths under NPC English publication of PIPL  
- Registry `primary_url` points at a stable official/portal entry for refresh

## When to suggest

Processing personal information of natural persons **in the PRC**; offering
products/services to individuals in the PRC; analyzing/evaluating behaviour of
individuals in the PRC (extraterritorial triggers under Art. 3 area).
