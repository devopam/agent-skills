# Pack: privacy-lc (Saint Lucia)

**Primary:** Data Protection Act (Revised Laws of Saint Lucia)  
**Runnable:** yes with **strict commencement gate**

## Gate (unchanged after re-check)
Act comes into force on date(s) fixed by Minister by Order in the Gazette; different Parts may commence on different dates. **Verify which Parts are in force** before asserting registration, Commissioner, or rights duties.

T1 Principles · T2 Consent / sensitive data · T3 Rights (access, rectification) · T4 Registration of controllers · T5 Transfers · T6 Commissioner powers
