# Scope and boundaries

## In scope

- Suggest **candidate regimes** from user answers + repo signals
- Confirm packs with the user before deep analysis
- Map **obligation themes** to primary article/section cites
- Score or label repo evidence: present / partial / missing / unknown
- Severity-ordered gaps + remediation directions (not full policy drafts)
- Optional `docs/compliance-baseline.md` for drift

## Out of scope

- Statements that the project **is compliant** or certified
- Formal legal advice or representation before a regulator
- Continuous monitoring of every jurisdiction worldwide
- Filling out regulatory filings or DPIAs as attorney work product
- Replacing counsel, DPO, or auditor sign-off

## Required disclaimer (skill runtime)

Every report must state that output is an **applicability and gap-orientation
aid**, may be incomplete, and must be verified against official texts and
qualified advisors before reliance.

## Evidence rule

- Cite only articles/sections listed in the active pack’s obligation cards or
  the source registry.
- Do not invent article numbers.
- Prefer primary official URLs; secondary sources only as interpretation aids.
