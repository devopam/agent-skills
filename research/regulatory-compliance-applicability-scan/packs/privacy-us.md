# Research: privacy-us (Americas — United States)

**Snapshot:** 2026-09-15

## Nature of US privacy law

There is **no single federal GDPR-equivalent**. Pack must:

1. Gate on **state comprehensive privacy** where signals match (start: **California CCPA/CPRA**)
2. Separately gate **sectoral federal** rules only when domain packs apply (HIPAA under healthcare; GLBA etc. under fintech research)

Do **not** invent a unified “US Privacy Act” checklist.

## Primary sources (California — first country/state depth)

| Field | Value |
|-------|--------|
| CCPA as amended (CPRA) | California Civil Code Title 1.81.5, roughly §§ 1798.100 et seq. |
| Official code | [California Legislative Information — Civil Code](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/) (e.g. § 1798.100) |
| Agency | California Privacy Protection Agency (CPPA) — regulations/guidance = interpretation aid |

### Theme anchors (illustrative Civil Code)

- Notice at collection / purpose limitation: § 1798.100
- Consumer rights (access, delete, correct, opt-out of sale/share): §§ 1798.105–1798.125 range
- Sensitive personal information: definitions § 1798.140; use limitations
- Service provider / contractor contracts: definitions and contractual requirements
- Enforcement: civil/administrative paths via statute + CPPA
