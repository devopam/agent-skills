## Scope of this pack

**In:** Union-level GDPR obligations common across EU/EEA.  
**Out:** Member-state derogations, national employment/health statutes,
national cookie/ePrivacy implementations — see
[08-eu-member-state-variations.md](../08-eu-member-state-variations.md).

When the project is clearly DE/FR/IT-centric, pair with overlay packs once they
exist; otherwise state national nuance **Not covered**.
