# Research: domain-healthcare-pharma

**Snapshot:** 2026-09-15

## Role

**Overlay** on regional privacy packs when health or pharma personal data is in play.

## Primary sources (v0 orientation)

| Region | Source | Notes |
|--------|--------|--------|
| US | **HIPAA** Privacy / Security / Breach — [45 CFR Part 164 (eCFR)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/subtitle-A/subchapter-C/part-164); HHS OCR pages | Applies to **covered entities** and **business associates** regarding **PHI** — gate carefully; not every health app is HIPAA |
| EU | GDPR Art. 9 special categories + national health rules | Health data under GDPR; **national** health laws Not covered until researched |
| India | DPDP + any sector health rules | Sector overlays **Not covered** until primary sources registered |
| ME / others | **Not covered** until researched |

## HIPAA theme anchors (when US PHI + CE/BA applies)

- Privacy Rule uses/disclosures: 45 CFR 164 Subpart E
- Security Rule (ePHI): Subpart C
- Breach notification: Subpart D (verify current text)
- BA contracts: organizational requirements

## Repo signals

PHI, EHR, HL7/FHIR, “HIPAA”, patient portal, clinical trial app, BA agreement templates, NPI, covered entity language.

## Explicit limits

If no PHI/CE/BA signals, do not force HIPAA. Consumer wellness without CE may still trigger **regional privacy** only.
