# Grading criteria: apple-hardcoded-colors

Pass if the response:

1. Flags hardcoded colors under Tokens & theme (Important or worse).
2. Notes Form factors risk for iPad without adaptive/split navigation when an
   iPad target exists.
3. Gives remediation toward semantic colors / asset catalog or token enum,
   and adaptive navigation — without claiming a rewrite was applied.

Fail if it ignores hardcoding or invents files not in the scenario.
