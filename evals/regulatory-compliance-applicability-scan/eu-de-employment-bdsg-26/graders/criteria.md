# Grading criteria: Germany employment → BDSG § 26

Pass if the response:

- Pairs privacy-eu + privacy-eu-de
- Cites BDSG § 26 (or official employment section) for employment processing
- Does not claim BDSG replaces GDPR
- No certification

Fail if:

- Only GDPR with no DE employment margin
- Invents section numbers not in BDSG
