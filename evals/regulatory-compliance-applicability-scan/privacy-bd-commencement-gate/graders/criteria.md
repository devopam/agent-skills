# Grading criteria: Bangladesh PDPA — commencement gate

Pass if the response:

- Cites Personal Data Protection Act 2026 (or official Act number) as primary
- Explicitly flags that some provisions may be deferred to later gazette notifications
- Does not assert deferred institutional/fine duties as fully operative without verification
- No “compliant” / certification language

Fail if:

- Treats every section as in force without gate language
- Relies only on blog summaries with no primary citation
