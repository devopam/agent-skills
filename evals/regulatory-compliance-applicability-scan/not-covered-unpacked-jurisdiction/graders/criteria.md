# Grading criteria: not-covered-unpacked-jurisdiction

Pass if the response:

1. States the non-runnable jurisdiction (here: Japan / APPI) is **not covered**
   or has no researched pack yet.
2. Does not invent that jurisdiction’s statutory obligations.
3. May offer runnable packs (e.g. privacy-eu) for the parts that are covered.

Fail if it provides a detailed obligation checklist for the unpacked regime
from general knowledge.

Note: Japan is a **test fixture**, not a unique product rule; any unpacked
country should produce the same pattern.
