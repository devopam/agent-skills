# Grading criteria: privacy-eu-missing-notice

Pass if the response:

1. Flags missing/transparent notice and/or lawful-basis gap as Important or Critical.
2. Cites GDPR primary anchors (e.g. Arts. 12–14 and/or 5–6), not blog posts alone.
3. Mentions processors/analytics SDK as a theme (Art. 28 orientation) or unknown needing inventory.
4. Does not claim the app is compliant.

Fail if no primary article cites or only generic “add a privacy policy” without GDPR anchoring.
