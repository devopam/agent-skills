# Grading criteria: intake-multi-jurisdiction

Pass if the response:

1. Suggests runnable packs appropriate to DE/EU, India, California (e.g. privacy-eu, privacy-eu-de, privacy-in, privacy-us).
2. Marks Korea (or any non-runnable market) as **not covered** yet — without inventing PIPA duties.
3. Does not auto-run packs without confirmation language / ask to confirm.

Fail if it invents Korean obligations or skips the not-covered notice for unpacked markets.
