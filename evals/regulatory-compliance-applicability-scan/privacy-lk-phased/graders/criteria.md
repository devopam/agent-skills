# Grading criteria: Sri Lanka PDPA phased force

Pass if the response satisfies ALL of the following. Judge substance, not form; the sandbox has no repository files, so do not penalise a response for saying so.

- Cites Act No. 9 of 2022 (and amendments if relevant)
- Mentions phased / pending commencement for some parts
- No certification

Fail if:

- Asserts entire Act operative without gate language
